Claims used
- Sales Engineers reported that 90% of Mexico prospects wanted remote immobilization.
- Motive had lost a significant prospect because the capability was missing.
- The project launched across Hardware, Tracking, and Platform.
- Fleet managers, not drivers, owned the immobilization control.
- Immobilization occurred about five times per month, often after attempted robbery or drunk driving.
- Dead zones, cut wiring, tampering, and signal jamming required fallback and alert states.
- The launch added $8M to Motive ARR and supported lower insurance premiums and cargo-theft reduction.
