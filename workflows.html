Workflows

A unified workflow management tool that lets you track PRDs, tasks, projects, and designs without leaving the platform.

Role

Product Design

Duration

8 months

Team

Design, PM, Engineering

The problem

The team grew from 50 to more than 600 people.

As our team at Educative scaled from 50 people to over 600, a centralized repository of projects, communication, and documentation became necessary.

The existing tools solved separate parts of the problem, which meant constant context switching and upkeep. We needed one place for multiple teams, so we started building Workflows.

My role

I worked across design, product, engineering, and sales.

By this point, I had been at Educative for over two years. It was an ambitious product that our leadership was heavily focused on, and I worked directly with sales, engineering, and product leadership on the end-to-end process.

Besides this, I was also involved in hiring and developing an outcome-focused team of designers and illustrators.

Research

I spoke with 31 people about how they worked.

I spoke with 31 people, including 18 Engineering Managers and 13 Software Engineers, recruited through my LinkedIn network.

Since this was an exploratory phase, I focused on how they used existing work-management tools, what they liked, what they disliked, and where the gaps existed.

Key insights

  • “I can never figure out project progress.”Engineering Managers have tight schedules and don’t want to search for documents or projects.
  • “Design, PM, and dev docs get lost.”A person has to search four separate platforms depending on what they are looking for: Slite.com for product, Monday.com for task tracking, GitHub for PR reviews, and Figma for design.
  • “Signoffs become blockers and I have to remind people via Slack.”People forget things, and PMs constantly remind via messaging apps.
  • “Discussions on technical docs are difficult to resolve.”Reviewers treated communication on the pages as low priority because they used Monday.com to manage the work.

Final designs

  • Task ManagerA complete end-to-end task manager. Tasks are associated with projects and documents and can be assigned to anyone.
  • Review ManagerRequest reviews on documents from any team member, with full review flow, status tracking, and resolution.
  • Project ManagerA project contains several tasks and documents. It can have a due date and multiple collaborators.
  • Document EditorA live multiplayer document editor with auto-save. Tasks and reviews are embedded in-document with clear status.

Outcome

The work helped land a $100K deal.

Workflows brought projects, tasks, reviews, and documentation into one place.
