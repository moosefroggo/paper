Engine Immobilizer

Remote immobilization for fleet managers responding to vehicle theft.

Role

Product Design Lead

Duration

2 months

Team

Design, PM, Engineering, Hardware

The problem

Motive needed remote immobilization to enter the Mexican market.

When Motive started expanding to Mexico in Q3 2023, the feedback was clear: 90% of prospects asked for remote immobilization. We’d already lost deals over it. This went from a nice-to-have feature to a blocker between us and an entire market.

We had until the end of Q4 to figure it out and ship in Q1 2024.

Why this was hard

The feature had to fit four platform changes.

The timing was brutal. Admin was mid-migration from 1.0 to 2.0, and the Vehicles page we needed to redesign hadn’t been rebuilt yet. Fleet View was getting new map icons simultaneously. A Device Hub was in early development that would eventually own all device controls.

We were also sourcing hardware from a third-party vendor for the first time, which limited what device data we could surface and how it communicated with our systems.

Customer research

Fleet managers made the decision.

  • “Fleet managers, not drivers, control immobilization.” The decision to kill the engine sits with the manager, not the driver.
  • “Vehicles get immobilized roughly five times a month.” The top reasons were attempted robbery and drunk driving.
  • “Tampering is a real fear.” Thieves know where devices are installed and can damage the wiring to disable the system.
  • “Dead zones on highways are a problem.” If there’s no signal, the device is useless and customers wanted a fallback.

Design decisions

We tested five places for the control.

Placement was the critical decision. I tested five different positions: action bar, below vehicle status, next to it, a dedicated section, and a persistent banner.

The banner won because immobilized vehicles are rare, appearing in less than 1% of fleets. It stood out without cluttering the normal view. Fleet View became a real-time status page, and the first release covered the starter line. Fuel line deceleration was left for a later release.

What shipped

  • Immobilized BannerA persistent banner on the vehicle detail page showing who immobilized the vehicle, when, and from where.
  • Live TrackingReal-time location data, live video feed, and follow mode to track stolen vehicles. Immobilized vehicles surface at the top of the fleet list.
  • Trip TimelineImmobilization events logged on the vehicle’s trip timeline, alongside dashcam footage for full context.
  • Tamper & Jammer AlertsPost-launch alerts for physical device removal and signal jamming, added based on direct customer feedback.

Impact

Added $8M to Motive’s ARR.

Engine Immobilizer opened the Mexican market for Motive and added $8M to ARR. Customers reported lower insurance premiums and a measurable drop in cargo theft.
